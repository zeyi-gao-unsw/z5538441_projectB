Validation of the project's sector news-sentiment index (VADER + a custom finance lexicon) against the course's finVADER benchmark (VADER + SentiBigNomics x0.1 + Henry 2008 word list) on identical headlines. Panel A overlays the two lagged daily indices for the highest-, median- and lowest-correlation sectors; panel B shows the per-sector Pearson correlation. Both indices use the same pipeline: ticker-day average, 5-day EMA, equal-weight sector average, forward-fill up to 10 trading days, lagged 1 trading day. Sample period: 02 Jan 2020 - 29 Dec 2023.
